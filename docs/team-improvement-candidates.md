# Inactive Team Improvement Candidates

These are research candidates, not current instructions. No runtime, adapter or
prompt loader imports this document. Changes require an explicitly frozen treatment
and matched evaluation; keeping an idea here does not establish its usefulness.

Removed from the active baseline during the 2026-11-01 review:

- Structured briefs carrying original requirements, unchecked assumptions, missing
  prerequisites, acceptance criteria and evidence locations.
- Instructions not to prescribe an interpretation before source inspection, and to
  distinguish reported conclusions from independently inspected evidence.
- Evidence-aware handoffs that separate production, self-checks, independent checks,
  unresolved assumptions and unsupported claims.
- Task-tuned estimand guidance, including the archeology endpoint-versus-average
  ambiguity. This is contaminated diagnostic material, not a task-neutral default;
  do not reinstate it based on the known target answer.
- Coordination guidance about source-inventory ownership, avoiding redundant work,
  retaining source artifacts and integrating saved results rather than summaries.

Exact artifact identity is now enforced uniformly by path-based submission, not by
asking a Leader to copy source text or obey a no-rewriting sentence. This transport
contract does not replace a future, separately specified execution evaluator.

The user's subsequent v3 baseline decision activates a generic units/precision/
coverage/joins/reproducibility checklist in `PROJECT_CONTEXT` for Single and Workers.
It is no longer an inactive candidate or a root-only checklist. The task-tuned
archeology estimand guidance above remains inactive.

There is deliberately no read-only-after-wakeup candidate or configuration. Message
activations preserve the member's original grants, as requested by the user.
